The Task Board

Everything you need to run your week on it, in about ten minutes.

Use the arrow keys, or the buttons at the bottom.

Why it exists

One place, one version of the truth

Every task for every client lives here. Who owns it, what state it is in, when it is due, and the whole conversation about it.

The point is not admin. The point is that nobody has to ask "where are we with this" ever again.

If work is not on the board, it does not exist. That rule only works if everyone follows it, including for the small stuff.

Getting in

tasks.wecomeone.me

Your own email and password. Nothing is visible until you sign in.

Forgot it? Type your email on the sign in screen and click Forgot your password. A reset link arrives in a minute and lasts an hour.

It works on your phone. Same address, same login. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

The three views

Same tasks, three ways of looking

  • Dashboard. The morning view. Counts, progress per client, who is carrying what, and anything that needs attention.
  • Board. The working view. Columns by status, drag a card across as work moves.
  • Table. The detail view. Every field, sortable by any column. Good for a client by client sweep.

The filter bar at the top applies to all three, so filtering to one client stays filtered as you switch.

Inside a task

The fields that matter

  • Client and project. Project groups tasks, a campaign or a retainer month.
  • Assignee. Who is responsible, not who is doing it right now.
  • Due date. Drives the overdue flags and the daily email. Leave it empty if there is genuinely no deadline.
  • Brief. What done looks like.
  • Links. One per line. The drive folder, the brief, the live page.

The important slide

What the six statuses mean

  • Not startedAgreed and captured. Nobody has begun.
  • In progressSomeone on our team is actively working on it now.
  • WaitingParked with someone outside the team. A contractor, the client, a supplier.
  • BlockedStuck. It needs a decision or something we do not have.
  • ReviewFinished, waiting on approval. This emails Andreas.
  • CompletedDone and delivered.

The one people get wrong

In progress means us. Waiting means them.

If a contractor has the website, or the client owes us photos, that is Waiting, not In progress. Leaving it as In progress makes it look like we are working on it when nobody is.

Set Waiting and the panel asks who has it. The board then counts the days.

Past a week the chip turns red and the task appears in Needs attention, so it stops being forgotten.

Moving work along

Drag on a laptop, tap on a phone

On the board, drag a card into another column. The start date fills in the first time it leaves Not started.

On a phone there is a status dropdown on every card, since dragging does not work on a touch screen.

Put a date in the Completed field and the status changes to Completed by itself. You never need to do both.

Completed tasks drop off the board after thirty days. They are still there, use Show archive in the filter bar.

Talking about work

Comments, not messages

Every task has a comment thread. Use it instead of WhatsApp for anything about that task.

Six weeks later the reasoning sits with the task rather than three hundred messages up a chat.

Comment on someone else's task and they get an email. Comment on your own and nobody is disturbed.

Email

When the board writes to you

  • A task is assigned to you
  • A task is handed over to you
  • Someone comments on your task
  • A task moves to Review, to the admin
  • Each morning, anything of yours due that day

Nothing else. Status changes, edits and moved dates are silent on purpose, so the emails stay worth reading.

The habit

Two minutes, twice a day

Morning. Open the Dashboard. Read Needs attention. Anything overdue, blocked, urgent, or parked too long is right there.

End of day. Move what you finished, park what you handed on, add what came up.

A board updated once a week is worse than no board, because people trust it and it is wrong.

The My tasks button filters to yours. Overdue only shows the fires.

Admin only

Things only Andreas can do

  • Delete tasks. Everyone else can do everything except delete, which is what stops accidents. It asks before it does it.
  • Add people. Supabase, Authentication, Invite user. They set their own password.
  • Backups. Under the avatar menu, Export a backup. Worth doing monthly.

Tasks assigned to an admin are private and invisible to everyone else. Bear that in mind before handing a task to Andreas rather than moving it to Review.
